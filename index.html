About

I am a software engineer and network specialist with a BSc in Software Engineering (GPA 19.84/20), currently transitioning into artificial intelligence. My professional background lies in enterprise and data center network architecture, where I analyze legacy infrastructures, model their structural relationships, and contribute to the design of modern architectures alongside senior engineers.

My research interests sit at the intersection of AI systems, secure computing and distributed infrastructure. I am particularly interested in large language models (LLMs), their practical application through tools such as Claude, and the security and infrastructure requirements for deploying them reliably, including post-quantum cryptography, on which I have published.

Scientific knowledge evolves continuously; I treat ongoing study as a professional obligation.
